# Revenue Management Intake Questionnaire

## Property and scope

- Property name and internal property ID (no guest or employee identifiers)
- Location, timezone, currency, opening/renovation dates
- Total sellable rooms by room type
- Analysis as-of date and historical/future horizon
- Direct booking, channel, and rate-plan scope
- Primary commercial objective: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, profit, mix, or balanced scorecard

## Operating context

- PMS/CRS/RMS and available exports or API access
- Room inventory, out-of-order, maintenance blocks, group blocks, and closure dates
- Known events, compression dates, holidays, weather-sensitive periods, and local demand drivers
- Comp set definition, source, collection frequency, and data licensing constraints
- Current BAR ladder, fences, restrictions, overrides, and approval process

## Data request

- Property configuration
- Reservation facts, including historical and on-books records
- Daily inventory and availability
- Market/comp rates by stay date and room type, if available
- Optional event calendar, booking-source mapping, and rate-plan mapping

## Governance

- Who may approve recommendations?
- Where are approved rates published?
- What changes require a second approver?
- How should overrides, rejected recommendations, and outcomes be recorded?
- Retention and deletion requirements for exports

## PII boundary

Provide only aggregated or pseudonymous commercial facts. Exclude guest names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, payment details, loyalty IDs, free-text notes, room numbers, employee names, and employee contact details.
