# Human Review Gate

Before operational use, a named approver must review:

- the as-of date and input freshness;
- data-quality warnings and missing signals;
- rate floors, ceilings, restrictions, and event assumptions;
- high-impact recommendations, insufficient history and provisional scenarios;
- any manual override and its expiry date.

The approval record should include recommendation ID, decision, reviewer, timestamp, reason, and resulting published value. The toolkit does not publish changes itself.
