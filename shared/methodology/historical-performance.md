# Historical Performance Methodology

This is analyst guidance for deriving descriptive metrics from supplied evidence. The offline review engine does not produce a complete historical performance audit.

Analyze daily and aggregated ADR, occupancy, RevPAR, room nights, revenue, cancellation rate, channel mix, room-type mix, lead time, length of stay, and booking-window behavior.

Segment in this order: property, room type, stay date, day of week, season/month, channel, rate-plan family, and occupancy band. Collapse a segment when sample size is weak. Use medians and p25/p75 ranges for rate and pace diagnostics.

The goal is to find repeatable patterns and exceptions: high-value dates, underpriced compression, weak shoulders, displacement risk, excessive discounting, and channel or process leakage. Every conclusion must distinguish correlation from causation.
